How to Create a Quiz

Follow these steps to create a quiz and assign it to your class:

  • Click on "Create Quiz" from your dashboard.
  • Fill in the quiz title, instructions, duration, and passing marks.
  • Select the class you want to assign it to.
  • Select the time in which you want the quiz to start/available to learners.
  • When you click notify learners, learners will get the email alert as soon as you submit the quiz.
  • When you click publish immediately the learners will not get the marks after they submit the quiz, they will only get the marks when you publish them on "VIEW QUIZZES".
  • Add questions one by one, specifying correct answers and options, lastly add the number of points per question.
  • You can choose how many times you want the learners to take the quiz.
  • We are aware that some schools cannot test their learners using the quiz system because they do not have computer rooms, however we have added the manual system so that the teacher can add marks in the system.
  • The teacher can manually add marks by adding a quiz on learner grades.
  • If you have cool ideas for the platform or you are experiencing abnormal behaviour please contact our team at support@iskool.xyz

How to View Quiz Results

To see how your learners performed:

  • Click on "Learner Grades" on your dashboard.
  • Select a quiz to view detailed marks and attempt history.

Uploading Study Resources

Support your class with notes, past papers, or any helpful files:

  • Click "Resources" from your dashboard.
  • Choose "Upload Resource" and fill in the title and file.
  • Assign the resource to a class.
  • You can also click "View My Uploads" to manage existing files.

Sending Announcements

Share updates with learners and/or parents:

  • Click on "Announcements".
  • Choose "Make Announcement".
  • Select the class and whether to send to learners, parents, or both.
  • Type your message and submit.

Managing Your Classes

When you create a quiz or upload a resource, you assign it to a class. All learners in those classes will receive what you share with them.

Online Assignments

When you upload an assignment, all learners in the class will receive it. Learners can also submit assignments back online for the teacher to view or download.
