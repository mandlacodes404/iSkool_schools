Taking Quizzes

Access and complete quizzes assigned by your teacher:

  • Click "Take a Quiz" to find and complete quizzes your teacher has assigned.

Viewing Results

Check your quiz performance and scores:

  • After taking a quiz, check your scores under "Quiz Results".
  • You can only see your marks once the teacher has published them.

Joining a Class

Join your class to access quizzes, resources, and announcements:

  • Select your class using "Join a Class". You can only do this once.
  • "My Class" shows the class you've joined. Contact admin if you need to switch, or email support@iskool.online.

Accessing Resources

Use study materials provided by your teacher:

  • Teachers upload notes and study guides in "Study Resources" for your learning.

Viewing Announcements

Stay updated with teacher announcements:

  • Teachers may post updates in "Announcements" — check regularly, but you’ll also get email alerts.
