Programme terms

What partners need to know

These terms govern the partner relationship and apply to all approved partners from the date of their agreement.

💰

Pricing is fixed

The subscription rate — R125 per learner per year — is set by iSkool and is not open to negotiation. Partners may not offer discounts or alternative pricing to schools.

🏷️

Referrals must be registered first

Every referral must be logged through your unique partner code before any engagement with the school begins. Referrals made outside this process cannot be tracked or paid.

🏫

One partner per school

The first registered referral takes precedence. If a school has already been registered by another partner, you will be notified before any further engagement takes place.

📅

Commission paid on confirmed payment

Commission is processed within 30 days of the school's subscription payment clearing. For pilot conversions, this begins at first payment — not during the pilot itself.

🤝

Accurate representation required

Partners may not make commitments beyond what iSkool has agreed to. Misrepresentation of the product, its pricing, or its capabilities is grounds for termination of the partnership.

♾️

No expiry on commission

There is no time limit on commission from a referred school. Commission continues to be paid on every annual renewal for as long as the school's subscription remains active.
