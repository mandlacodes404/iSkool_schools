Setup Guide

Get the iSkool app on your phone

Follow these steps to install iSkool and turn on notifications — so you never miss a quiz, assignment, or announcement from your teacher.

Part 1 — Add iSkool to your Home Screen
1
Open Safari on your iPhone
iSkool must be installed through Safari — not Chrome or any other browser. Find the Safari icon (blue compass) on your home screen and open it.
👆 Tap the Safari icon
2
Go to your school's iSkool link
Type your school's link in the Safari address bar at the top and press Go. It looks like: yourschool.iskool.online

Scroll and tap "Add to Home Screen"
In the Share Sheet, scroll across the icons until you see "Add to Home Screen" (house icon). Tap it.

Part 2 — Turn on Notifications
1
Open iSkool from your home screen icon
Tap the iSkool icon on your home screen — not Safari. It must be opened from the icon for notifications to work on iPhone.
👆 Tap the iSkool icon on your home screen
2
Log in and go to Settings
After logging in, tap the ⚙ gear icon in the top right corner of your dashboard to open Settings.

Part 1 — Add iSkool to your Home Screen
1
Open Google Chrome on your Android phone
iSkool works best through Google Chrome on Android. Open Chrome from your home screen or app drawer.
👆 Tap the Chrome icon
2
Go to your school's iSkool link
Tap the address bar, type your school's link and press Go: yourschool.iskool.online
3
Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top right
Once the page loads, tap the three vertical dots in the top right corner of Chrome to open the browser menu.

👆 Tap ⋮ then "Add to Home screen"
4
Keep the name as "iSkool" and tap Add
A popup appears showing the app name. Leave it as "iSkool" and tap Add to confirm. The icon will appear on your home screen.
👆 Tap "Add" to confirm
💡
Tip — Chrome may show an automatic banner!Chrome sometimes shows an "Install app" banner at the bottom of the screen automatically. You can tap that instead of using the ⋮ menu — both work perfectly.
