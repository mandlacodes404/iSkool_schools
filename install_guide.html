Setup Guide

Get the iSkool app on your phone

Follow the steps below to install iSkool and turn on notifications — so you never miss a quiz, assignment, or announcement.

Part 1 — Add iSkool to your Home Screen
1
Open Safari on your iPhone
iSkool must be installed through Safari — not Chrome or any other browser. Look for the Safari icon on your home screen (blue compass icon).
👆
Tap the Safari icon
2
Go to your school's iSkool link
Type your school's iSkool address in the Safari address bar at the top and press Go. It will look something like:

Part 2 — Turn on Notifications
1
Open iSkool from your home screen
Tap the iSkool icon you just added to your home screen to open the app. Do not open it through Safari — it must be opened from the icon.
👆
Tap the iSkool icon on your home screen
2
Log in to your account
Enter your school email and password to log in. If you don't have your login details, ask your teacher.
3
Tap "Allow" when asked about notifications
After logging in, iSkool will ask if it can send you notifications. Tap "Allow" so you get alerts for new quizzes, assignments and announcements.
🔔
"iSkool" Would Like to Send You Notifications
