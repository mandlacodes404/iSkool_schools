About Us

Who we are

Siiba M Loans is a South African micro-lending business committed to responsible, short-term credit. We believe that access to fair financial help should be straightforward — not buried in red tape or confusing contracts. Our focus is on affordability, honesty, and treating every client with dignity and respect.

We operate within the guidelines of the National Credit Act (NCA) and are committed to responsible lending practices. Loans are subject to affordability assessment and credit checks.
