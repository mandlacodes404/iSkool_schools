About Us

Who we are

Siiba M Loans is a South African micro-lending business committed to responsible, short-term credit. We believe access to fair financial help should be straightforward — not buried in red tape or confusing contracts.

We operate within the guidelines of the National Credit Act (NCA). All loans are subject to affordability assessment and credit checks.
