About Sonnet

We build what
the market forgot.

Sonnet is a South African software company focused on building category-defining products for underserved markets — starting with education, and growing from there.

Who we are

We don't build for
crowded markets.
We build for blind spots.

Sonnet was founded on a simple observation: the most impactful software gets built for the people that enterprise tech companies ignore. The schools without solutions. The communities without platforms. The industries that get copy-pasted tools from other industries instead of something purpose-built.

We're a small team with high standards. Every product we ship is the result of deep research into a specific market, obsessive product thinking, and a genuine belief that the people we're building for deserve world-class software — regardless of where they are in the world.

iSkool is our first product. It won't be our last.

Our mission

"Find the gaps. Build in the blue oceans. Create clarity where there was none."

Our principles
01
Start with the problem, not the solution

We don't start by asking "what can we build?" We start by asking "what's broken, who's affected, and why hasn't anyone fixed it?" Every product at Sonnet begins with months of listening before a single line of code is written.

02
Build for the people others overlook

The most interesting markets are the ones that established companies haven't bothered with — because the addressable market looks too small, too local, or too niche. That's exactly where we want to be. The overlooked is where the opportunity lives.

03
Fewer things, done properly

We'd rather ship one deeply considered product than five rushed ones. Quality over velocity. Depth over breadth. We believe the best software feels like it was made specifically for you — and that requires restraint as much as ambition.

04
Context is everything

A South African school is not an American school. A township learner is not a Silicon Valley user. Software that works in one context doesn't automatically work in another. We build with local context baked in from the start — not bolted on afterwards.

05
Honest, direct, no noise

We don't do vanity features, buzzword-driven roadmaps, or inflated promises. We build what users actually need, communicate clearly about what's coming, and own our shortcomings. If something's not right, we say so — and we fix it.

06
Long-term thinking over short-term metrics

We're building companies that last. Not products optimised for a fundraising deck or a quick acquisition. The communities we serve deserve builders who are in it for the long haul — and that's exactly who we are.

How we work

We take the road less
automated.

Every market we enter gets the same treatment: deep field research, real conversations with real users, and a product built from scratch to fit the context. No repurposed tools. No shortcuts.

Step 01
Deep market research

We spend months understanding a market before we touch a keyboard. User interviews, shadowing, and competitive analysis to understand what's really broken.

Step 02
Context-first design

Every design decision is filtered through the real-world constraints of the people we're building for — connectivity, device types, literacy levels, and daily workflows.

Step 03
Pilot with real users

We never launch cold. We run structured pilots with real institutions, gather feedback obsessively, and iterate before scaling.

Step 04
Build for the long term

Once we've validated fit, we build infrastructure for scale — without compromising the simplicity that made the product work in the first place.
