The idea

Turning operations into intelligence

Every organization generates an extraordinary amount of information every day. Every transaction, interaction, record, and report tells part of a much larger story. Yet for most organizations, this information remains fragmented across departments, spreadsheets, and disconnected software.

Laura was created to change that. Developed by Enthropy, Laura is an organizational intelligence platform that transforms operational data into meaningful, real-time insight — enabling every level of the organization, from individual teams to executive leadership, to understand performance, identify challenges, and make better-informed decisions.

Laura does not replace an organization's existing systems — it builds upon them. As teams use the tools they already rely on to manage their day-to-day work, Laura continuously transforms that operational data into intelligence that helps leaders understand what is happening, why it is happening, and where action is needed.

Privacy & responsible intelligence

Every user sees only what they're authorised to see

Organizational data belongs to the organizations that generate it. Laura is designed with role-based permissions and responsible data governance at its core — security, privacy, and organizational trust are foundational, not optional.

Team level

Managers see their teams

Access is scoped to what a manager is responsible for — their own people, their own team data, nothing beyond it.

Department level

Directors see their departments

A complete view of the department — every team, every trend — without visibility into other departments' data.

Regional & divisional level

Leaders see their jurisdiction

Regional leaders see their regions. Divisional leadership sees their divisions. Each tier's intelligence is scoped to its responsibility.

Enterprise level

Executives see aggregated intelligence

An organization-wide view built from aggregated data — informing strategy without compromising the privacy of any individual team or employee.
