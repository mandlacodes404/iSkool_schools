The idea

A campus deserves its own platform

Universities are complex communities. The Vice-Chancellor, Deans, faculties, student societies, the SRC, and campus media all need to reach students — but today they do it on platforms built for everyone: Instagram, X, email. Messages get lost. Posts compete with noise from the whole world.

Laura is a closed, verified university platform. Every stakeholder posts to their own audience — the campus — not to the internet at large. Students receive communications from the people who actually shape their university life, in one organised, trusted space.

And beyond announcements, Laura is where university life happens: societies are built here, groups are formed, members communicate, elections are run, and campus radio and news has a home.

Who Laura serves

Every voice on campus

Laura gives every tier of university life its own verified presence — so messages land with authority, context, and the right audience.

Vice-Chancellor & Leadership

Post congratulations, policy updates, and major announcements directly to the entire university community — no PR intermediaries, no platform algorithms.

Dean of Students

Wish students luck before exams. Announce support services, wellness initiatives, and academic calendar events — every student sees it, immediately.

Faculties & Departments

Engineering, Law, Commerce — each faculty has its own space to post to its students. Timetable changes, lecture cancellations, department news.

Student Representative Council

Run elections on Laura. Campaign to your fellow students. Recruit. Announce outcomes. The SRC's voice reaches every student without a single flyer or Instagram post.

University Societies

Build your society on Laura. Create a group, invite members, post updates, organise events. Members communicate within the society's own space — private, purposeful, campus-only.

Campus Radio & Media

Campus news and radio have a home on Laura — not buried in a Facebook group. The editorial team posts. Students tune in. Campus media finally reaches its own audience.

Imagine this

A university where the right message reaches the right people — without leaving campus.

Vice-Chancellor

Wants to thank the hockey team after an intervarsity win. Opens Laura, posts to the university feed. Every student sees it — in 30 seconds. No PR team, no Instagram caption, no email list.

Dean of Students

Wants to wish students luck before semester exams. Posts on Laura. Students get it. It feels personal — because it came directly from their Dean, on their campus platform.

Student Representative Council

SRC elections open. Candidates campaign. Students vote. All of it happens inside Laura — the whole campus participates without a single external link or flyer in sight.

Drama Society

Needs new members for the spring production. Posts on Laura's open feed and the Drama Society group. Interested students join the group, read updates, RSVP to auditions — all in one place.

Campus Radio

Uploads tonight's show notes and a listener poll. Students who follow Campus Radio on Laura see it immediately. The station has its first true campus audience.

Faculty of Law

A moot court date changes. The faculty posts to Law students only. No mass email to the whole university. The right people are informed — instantly.
