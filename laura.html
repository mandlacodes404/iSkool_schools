The idea

Turning operations into intelligence

Education generates an extraordinary amount of information every day. Every attendance register, assessment, assignment, classroom interaction, and academic milestone tells part of a much larger story. Yet for most education systems, this information remains fragmented across schools, spreadsheets, and disconnected software.

Laura was created to change that. Developed by Enthropy, Laura is an education intelligence platform that transforms operational data into meaningful, real-time insight — enabling every level of the education system, from individual schools to national departments, to understand performance, identify challenges, and make better-informed decisions.

Laura does not replace existing school operations — it builds upon them. As schools use iSkool to manage teaching, learning, communication, and administration, Laura continuously transforms that operational data into intelligence that helps education leaders understand what is happening, why it is happening, and where action is needed.

Privacy & responsible intelligence

Every user sees only what they're authorised to see

Educational data belongs to the institutions that generate it. Laura is designed with role-based permissions and responsible data governance at its core — security, privacy, and institutional trust are foundational, not optional.

Classroom level

Teachers see their classrooms

Access is scoped to what a teacher is responsible for — their own learners, their own classroom data, nothing beyond it.

School level

Principals see their schools

A complete view of the institution — every classroom, every trend — without visibility into other schools' data.

District & provincial level

Officials see their jurisdiction

District officials see their districts. Provincial departments see their provinces. Each tier's intelligence is scoped to its responsibility.

National level

National departments see aggregated intelligence

A system-wide view built from aggregated data — informing national policy without compromising the privacy of any individual school or learner.
